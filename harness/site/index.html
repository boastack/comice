Founded on the idea that media should react to the person looking at it, not just sit on the page. This paragraph wraps around the portrait's own ellipse rather than sitting in a rigid column — a real exercise of layout's shape-outside mechanism against actual flowing text, not a single short line. We work across identity, motion, and interactive product design for clients who want their sites to feel alive rather than static. Every project starts with the same question — what does this look like in motion, not just at rest — and every deliverable is judged against that answer before anything ships.
